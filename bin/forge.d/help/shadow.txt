Usage: forge shadow <slug> <feed>

Judge live shadow traffic after a /forge-port run. <slug> names the
port's judge, J = <state dir>/port/<slug>/; it must be locked, unchanged
since, and hold a cutover gate. <feed> is a file, or - for stdin, of
JSONL lines your mirror writes (Envoy request mirroring, GoReplay or the
service's own logging), one per live request answered by both the old
and the new code with the new code's side effects cut off:
  {"id": "<unique>", "at": "<ISO-8601 time with offset>",
   "request": "<text>", "old": "<old answer>", "new": "<new answer>"}

Each line whose id J has not recorded under the current lock is
compared as forge replay run compares, with J's locked rules, and
appended to J/shadow.jsonl as {id, at, lock, class}, a mismatch also
keeping request, old and new for the operator. A feed with a bad line
records nothing. It prints "shadow: <n> compared, <m> mismatches, <k>
already seen", one "class <paths> <count> <first id>" line per new
mismatch class, then the "window: ..." line forge cutover prints.

Exit codes:
  0  no new mismatch
  1  a new mismatch, a bad feed, a missing judge or gate
  2  bad arguments
  3  the judge is unlocked or changed since lock

Reads:
  <feed>, J/rules, J/lock, J/cutover, J/shadow.jsonl

Runs serialize on J/shadow.lock.

Writes:
  J/shadow.jsonl

Example:
  forge shadow receipts-rust /var/log/receipts/mirror.jsonl
