Usage: forge bg <name> [--cwd <dir>] [--env <KEY=VALUE>]... -- <cmd> [<arg>...]
       forge bg --stop <name>
       forge bg --active <name>

Start a named background job that outlives the calling session: a
systemd-run --user unit on Linux, a launchctl submit job on macOS, whose
output goes to ~/.local/state/forge/bg/<name>.log. The job gets the caller's
PATH. --stop stops it and exits 0 when it is not running; --active exits 0
while it runs.

Arguments:
  <name>   letters, digits and : _ . -
  --cwd    working directory of the job
  --env    one environment variable, repeatable

Example:
  forge bg forge-quick-fix-web --cwd ../web -- npm run dev
  forge bg --stop forge-quick-fix-web
