Usage: forge ready <track>/<feature>

Check that every feature <feature> depends on has shipped. A dependency
without a track prefix is in the same track.

Prints "ready" and exits 0, or prints "waiting on: <features>" and exits 1.
Exits 2 when the track has no such open feature.

Reads:
  .forge/tracks/*.json

Writes: nothing

Example:
  forge ready forge/agents
